# October 9, 2026 update

The publication includes SIDLocal **2.32.0** and the current Node source, with these changes from the earlier setup guides:

- Daily boards generate ten community milestones and six building milestones per real-world calendar day in America/Chicago. Community goals improve the village's appearance and function; building goals cover new construction and unfinished buildings. Cards are compact and adjacent, and the duplicate Town warehouse card was removed.
- Construction retains deferred blocks and unfinished projects, verifies the final building, and resumes work rather than abandoning partial shells. Authorized terrain preparation can level natural ground and reclaim bounded supported land while protecting existing structures and player edits.
- The existing jail was expanded to four equal cells, with four iron doors, four working levers, an accessible west entrance and a JAIL label.
- Percy Wobblefoot, nicknamed Tipsy, has a distinctive personality, witnessed disturbances, sheriff/court processing, short custody periods and a sober cooldown.
- Door recovery uses recent unfinished destinations and avoids pulling citizens toward stale goals. Intentional animal containment remains protected.
- Lamp-post construction replaces uncontrolled street lantern stacking, using spaced posts and hanging lanterns while preserving paths, crops and door approaches.
- Barnaby the Runner handles delivery requests. Jasper remains responsible for warehouse sorting and service.
- The Bank dashboard supports logged Mayor grants, personal donations, treasury/reserve transfers, and exact verified surplus reconciliation. Transaction IDs, persistent move markers, count verification and financial locks prevent duplicate funding and expose uncertain moves for review.

## Evidence and limits

At publication, the current Node suite and Java builds are rerun from this repository. See `verification/PUBLICATION.md` for the exact results.

Earlier live checks verified the four jail cells and all four door levers, the sheriff's west entrance traversal, Tink's controlled doorway entry/exit (entry used the automatic assistance fallback), and one supplied-material lamp post. These checks do not prove every home crossing or every future construction job. Natural terrain reclamation and a full unsupervised Tipsy disturbance-to-release cycle were not observed.

Bank checks found a pre-existing 512-emerald physical surplus. The Mayor approved assigning that verified surplus to wage reserves. The live audit then balanced at 2,346 physical emeralds and 2,346 recorded obligations, with 557 in reserves. A one-emerald warehouse transfer and its return both balanced; repeated transaction IDs did not move more coins. The original cause of the surplus was not established. Bank grants/donations and payroll use are covered by local tests; Chrome control timed out during visual bank verification.

Unfinished building recovery was still queued/in progress during the world inspections. Publication does not mean every supplied-photo building has finished construction. Raw world state, citizen bank accounts and conversations are excluded from the public repository.
